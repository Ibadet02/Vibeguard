## What VibeGuard is

VibeGuard scans a code repository for common security mistakes and produces a report. It combines deterministic pattern checks with an AI review. It is a tool to help you find problems, not a certification.

## No guarantee of security

- **A clean report does not mean your app is secure.** No scanner, human or AI, can find every vulnerability.
- The scanner can produce **false positives** (flagging things that are fine) and **false negatives** (missing real problems). The AI review in particular is probabilistic: it can misread code or be wrong with confidence.
- Reports are informational. They are not a security audit, penetration test, compliance assessment, or legal advice. Verify findings before acting on them, and do not rely on a scan as your only security measure.

## Your responsibilities
